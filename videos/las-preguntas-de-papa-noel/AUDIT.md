# "Las preguntas de Papá Noel": script audit (scheduled run, 2026-11-12, 20:00 Montevideo; arrived on time)

The first draft is reel 4 in `../PLAN_AJEDREZ.md` (2026-11-07). I audited it against:
- the rules that changed in `../RESEARCH_AJEDREZ_2_2026.md`;
- the synthesis in `../RESEARCH_AJEDREZ_2026.md`;
- the skill's lessons.

As asked, no new research team was used.

## Checklist

| Check | First draft | Verdict | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product named in words in frame 1 | Papá Noel holds up the viking king; hook "¿Cuánto sale?" | **Missing**: a non-player sees a man with a toy | Hook text "Ajedrez vikingo / impreso en 3D" from the first frame, over the set and Papá Noel |
| One joke, a comic engine | Four questions, four answers: a dry FAQ | **Missing** | **Papá Noel's famous list.** He reads the questions off a scroll; a counter "Pregunta 1/4 … 4/4" keeps score (a visible number, rule 1, and an open loop: viewers stay to see 4/4).

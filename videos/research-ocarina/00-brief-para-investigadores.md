# Shared context for every researcher (read first)

**The shop.** Creaciones del Mago is a one-person 3D-printing shop in Montevideo, Uruguay. It sells through Instagram
reels: short 3D toon animations (9:16, 20-25 s) with two recurring characters:
- El Mago, a kind, deadpan older wizard, the maker;
- Papá Noel, his jolly, impatient friend on holiday in Montevideo (it is summer there in December).
Orders go by WhatsApp: the price and date are given before starting, with no commitment. The last day to ORDER for
Christmas is 23 December 2026. That date is never a delivery promise.

**The new product.** A 12-hole transverse ocarina (the "sweet potato" shape), printed in 3D. From the owner's photos:
- matte black, teardrop-shaped, about hand length;
- about 10 finger holes on top, including two small "sub-holes" next to larger ones, and 2 thumb holes underneath;
- an angled mouthpiece and a rectangular window (the labium) near the mouthpiece;
- visible print layer lines.

Not yet known: the key (for example Alto C), the range, the material (PLA or PETG), the size in cm, the colours
available, whether it is tuned and tested before delivery, and whether a fingering chart or a cord comes with it. Never
assume any of these.

**Trademark rule (strict).** The 12-hole ocarina is strongly associated with a famous video-game franchise. In your
report NEVER write that franchise's name, its characters, its song titles or its game mechanics by name: write
"[the franchise]" instead. The shop will never use its name, melodies or logos, and never imply a licence. You MAY
note where buyer interest comes from that association, in those neutral words.

**Shop rules for the reels:**
- the product is named in words in frame 1;
- one joke that a non-player gets in 3 s and that proves a real feature;
- real photos as proof before ONE comment CTA ("Comentá X y te paso precio y fecha");
- honest urgency only;
- no prices, weights or finish claims on screen;
- first person singular, Rioplatense "vos";
- soft ambient music, with sound effects never on a spoken word.

**Evidence labels.** Tag EVERY claim:
- **[data]**: a study, an official source, a measured number, or a real quote from a buyer;
- **[secondary]**: an article, a blog, a vendor page, a search snippet;
- **[opinion]**: your own inference, or a maker's or forum opinion.
Give the source URL next to each claim. If a page did not load and you only saw a search snippet, say "(snippet)".
Most web sources are vendor and SEO blogs: say so when that is all you found. Never invent a URL, a number or a quote.

**Tools.** Use WebSearch (if it is not loaded, call ToolSearch with query "select:WebSearch" first; use mode
"standard" by default, "extended" only when standard is thin). Use WebFetch to read a page if you have it. Search in
English AND Spanish.

**Voice notes:**
- **"Y yo, caballero" was re-recorded.** A transcriber heard it as "hijo caballero", because in Rioplatense "y yo" sounds like "hijo". The new line is "Yo soy caballero".

- **Papá Noel's first take opened with a laugh,** which was cut off.

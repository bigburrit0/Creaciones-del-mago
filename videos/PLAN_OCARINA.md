# The 12-hole ocarina: four reels (scripts, 2026-11-14)

The research is in `RESEARCH_OCARINA_2026.md` (five Haiku angles; raw reports in `research-ocarina/`). The product is a
**12-hole transverse ocarina printed in 3D**: matte black, teardrop-shaped, about hand length, ten holes on top (two of
them small sub-holes) and two thumb holes underneath, an angled mouthpiece, visible layer lines.

**The rules stay the same:**
- the product is named in words in frame 1;
- one joke, which a non-player gets in 3 s and which proves a real feature;
- real photos, then ONE comment CTA that rewards the comment with price and date;
- honest urgency: the last day to order for Christmas is 23 December, never a delivery promise;
- no prices, weights, finish claims or trademarks;
- first person singular, Rioplatense "vos";
- soft ambient music, with effects never on a spoken word.

**What is new for the ocarina** (the full list is in the research, "Rules that change"):
1. **Real sound only.** Every note comes from the owner's recording, the bad-note gag included. A "Sonido real" badge
   shows while it plays. **No recording, no reel.**
2. **The ocarina is the music when it plays.** The ambient bed fades out before the first note and returns after the
   last. No line, subtitle or effect ever sits under a note.
3. **The mouthpiece never reaches a face** (beards, brims, the contact guard). The ocarina plays by El Mago's magic: his
   gesture plus sparkles on the holes. Papá Noel's attempts are POV inserts at chest height.
4. **Holes follow the real fingering** from the owner's chart. Without a chart, the sparkles circle the body.
5. **Nothing in the world reacts to the music** (no weather, light, time, doors, objects). No hooded figure, no lifting
   it to the camera, no puzzle chime. Only public-domain or original melodies. No franchise words or hashtags.
6. **Proof has a sound:** the owner's photo ("Así es de verdad"), then a real clip of the owner's hands playing
   ("Así suena de verdad").
7. **The black piece needs light:** day presets, a lit wall or the sky behind it, a rim light.

**Before any reel is built, the owner sends:** the three photos (and one of the ocarina in a hand), the recording (the
checklist is at the end), the key and range, the material, the size, and whether each piece is tested with a tuner.
Lines marked *(a confirmar)* stay out until then.

## Production notes (only when the owner asks for the reels; nothing is scheduled)

Each reel follows the production steps in `PLAN_NOVIEMBRE_2.md` (setup check, quick audit, voices first, ON_SCREEN.md,
the scene, the ambient music, preview frames, the QA gate, the render with an explicit cd, the post-render checks, copy,
wrap-up). These are the differences for the ocarina:

1. **The owner's material comes in first:**
   - the photos go to `videos/ocarina/fotos/`;
   - the recording and the clips go to `videos/ocarina/sonido/`;
   - large raw files stay out of git; commit trimmed WAVs and the short proof clips only.
2. **`notas.json`** (one per reel): for each note of the take used, the onset, the duration, the note name and its
   fingering (holes open and closed). Read it from the recording with a pitch tracker, check it by ear, and take the
   fingerings from the owner's chart. The hole sparkles and the open-hole states read only this file.
3. **The ocarina's audio:**
   - trim each phrase at clean silences;
   - remove the noise gently, using the owner's 5 s of silence as the profile;
   - normalise the phrase to about -18 LUFS, about 2 dB under the voices (the pure tone carries; opinion, to check by
     ear);
   - no reverb;
   - place it as `<audio data-who="ocarina" data-sub="no">` so no subtitle appears for it.
4. **Music cues:** the ambient bed in the ocarina's key (tonic from the owner's key, ionian; mixolydian for O3). Add a
   duck window with gain 0 from 0.5 s before each phrase to 0.3 s after it.
   - `nivel_musica.py` still applies to the voices.
   - Add one check: no voice clip, subtitle or effect overlaps an ocarina window.
5. **The proof clip:** the owner's video, cropped to 9:16 inside the "Así suena de verdad" frame, with its own audio as a
   separate clip and the bed at 0.
6. **QA additions:**
   - the floating ocarina is a prop: no teleports, and it never passes through a body, a mitten or the furniture;
   - the POV mittens never come within the face colliders;
   - the light never changes while the ocarina plays (rule 5).
7. **After the render:** transcribe the mix as usual, plus listen once from start to end for any word under a note.

## Para el dueño: cómo grabar la ocarina (lista para mandar)

1. Usá la grabadora de voz del celular (no una nota de voz de WhatsApp). Poné el celular en modo avión.
2. Buscá un cuarto con cortinas o sillón. Cerrá puertas y ventanas, y apagá el ventilador.
3. Poné el celular a unos 30 cm, un poco arriba de la ocarina y hacia un costado de la ventanita, nunca delante del
   soplido.
4. Hacé una prueba de 10 segundos con la nota más aguda y escuchala con auriculares. Si satura, alejá el celular.
5. Grabá 5 segundos de silencio al empezar.
6. Tocá la escala, lenta, de la nota más grave a la más aguda. Después, una nota larga sostenida.
7. Tocá el comienzo de "Oda a la alegría" y el comienzo de "Noche de paz", solo la melodía.
8. Tocá una frase corta tuya, inventada, alegre, de unos 8 segundos.
9. Soplá una nota fuerte a propósito, que suene fea (es para un chiste).
10. Grabá cada cosa dos o tres veces.
11. Filmá tus manos tocando la escala y "Noche de paz": de cerca, con luz de día, sin la cara si no querés.
12. Si la afinás, grabá la pantalla del afinador mientras tocás algunas notas.
13. Sacale una foto con la ocarina en la mano.
14. Mandame todo como archivo o documento. Si WhatsApp lo comprime, mandalo por Google Drive.

# Reel 4 · «La huerta de los pájaros» (simulador de granja)

**Resumen:** como en los juegos de granja, el Mago planta una semilla en el fondo de una casa de Montevideo, crece un
arbolito y, en vez de fruta, de la rama cuelga un comedero. Llegan un benteveo, un hornero y dos chingolos, y el Mago
se sienta con el mate. «Plantás esto. Cosechás esto.» Producto: comedero casita. 9:16, 10 s.

## PASO 2 · Generar el video completo con las imágenes

En Gemini, video **9:16 de 10 segundos**. Adjuntá las cuatro imágenes **en orden** (imagen 1, 2, 3 y 4) y pegá este
prompt:

```
Create one 10-second vertical 9:16 video using the four attached images as keyframes, in this order: Image 1 is the first frame (0 s), Image 2 appears at about 2 s, Image 3 at about 5 s, and Image 4 is the final frame (10 s). Keep exactly the look, the wizard, the birds, the feeder, the colors and the pixel art style of the images and animate smoothly from one to the next.

Single continuous shot, no cuts. Locked-off static camera, top-down three-quarter view like a 1990s 16-bit farming game, no zoom, no pan, no depth of field, no motion blur. Authentic 16-bit pixel art: hard square pixels, limited palette, low frame-rate sprite animation. Plants grow in distinct stage jumps like in a video game, not smoothly. Flat 2D for the whole clip.

Action:
0 to 2 seconds (Image 1 to Image 2): the wizard drops the glowing gold seed into the center plot and steps back; the plant grows in three quick stage jumps with a small sparkle each time: a green sprout, then a sapling, then a small young tree with round leaves.
2 to 5 seconds (Image 2 to Image 3): from the main branch, like a fruit growing, the pale lilac hexagonal bird feeder appears hanging from a short rope, full of seeds; the great kiskadee with the yellow belly flies in and lands on it; the rufous hornero flies in from the right.
5 to 8 seconds (Image 3 to Image 4): the hornero lands on the other side of the feeder; two small sparrows hop in and peck fallen seeds on the ground; the wizard unfolds the wooden chair on the right and sits down with a mate gourd.
8 to 10 seconds (Image 4): the birds keep pecking in a simple loop, the wizard sips the mate, the leaves sway slightly. The last second is calm.

Audio: a soft trowel tap, three rising pops as the plant grows, a cheerful harvest chime when the feeder appears, 8-bit wing flaps, a chiptune version of a kiskadee call, quick pecking ticks, a light happy chiptune melody in a major key, birdsong in the background. No dialogue, no voice, no music with lyrics.

No text, no letters, no numbers, no subtitles, no captions, no logos anywhere in the video.
```

**Si tu herramienta no acepta 4 imágenes:**
- Si acepta **primer y último cuadro**: usá la imagen 1 y la imagen 4, con el mismo prompt.
- Si acepta **una sola imagen**: usá la imagen 1, con el mismo prompt.
- Si el video sale de **8 s**: en la edición, congelá el último cuadro 2 s más.

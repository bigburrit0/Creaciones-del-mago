# The 12-hole ocarina: research for four reels (2026-11-14)

The owner, on 14/11/2026: "En otra session vamos a hacer una investigación para crear 4 scripts para este producto: es
una ocarina de 12 huecos." The scripts are in `PLAN_OCARINA.md`.

Five Haiku researchers worked on it, one angle each:
1. what buyers ask and fear;
2. ocarina content that performs on Reels and TikTok;
3. gift occasions and personas in Uruguay, late November to Reyes;
4. comedy formats with El Mago and Papá Noel;
5. sound, and how to show the real sound in a reel.

The brief they all got and their full reports are in `videos/research-ocarina/`. The franchise name was removed from two
URLs there.

Labels: **data** (a study, an official source, a measured number, or a real buyer's words), **secondary** (an article,
a vendor page or a search snippet), **opinion**.

**Limits.** This session could not open most of the sources:
- blocked: Prusa, Printables, Pure Ocarinas, STL Ocarina, Wikipedia, Meta's help pages, IMPO (the law's text), most
  Uruguayan press, Mercado Libre Uruguay;
- so most of the evidence below is **search snippets**, and most of it comes from vendors;
- no measured view counts for any ocarina or printed-instrument reel were found;
- no Spanish-language buyer questions were found.

Treat the numbers as starting points. The facts that matter most come from the owner, not from the web.

## The product (from the owner's description of the photos; the photos are not in the repository yet)

- A matte black, teardrop-shaped **transverse ocarina** (the shape is called "sweet potato" in English), about hand
  length, printed in 3D.
- About 10 finger holes on the front, two of them small "sub-holes" next to larger ones, and 2 thumb holes on the back:
  **12 holes**.
- An angled mouthpiece and a rectangular window (the labium) near it.
- Visible print layer lines.

**Not known yet (never invent them):** the key (for example Alto C), the range, the material (PLA or PETG), the size in
cm, the colours, whether each one is tuned and tested before delivery, whether a fingering chart or a cord comes with it,
and the lead time for one piece.

## 1. What buyers ask and fear (`research-ocarina/01-compradores.md`)

| Topic | Finding | Label and source |
|---|---|---|

| Tuning | Prusa's blog found its printed ocarina "very nicely in tune", with the A at 440 Hz | secondary, vendor blog, blog.prusa3d.com/3d-print-a-recorder-an-ocarina-or-a-kazoo-3d-pritned-musical-instruments_49390/ |

| Tuning | "Printing is a poor way to get precise tuning" (a forum comment) | opinion (forum), lr.us.psf.lt/r/Ocarina/comments/1fhbqzh |
| Key | The usual beginner's 12-hole is **Alto C** | secondary, flutetunes.com/articles/ocarina/, puresmusic.com/blogs/ocarinas/buying-your-first-ocarina |
| Range | Alto C 12-hole: about **A4 to F6**, an octave and a sixth. Sources vary by model. **No source supports "two octaves"** | secondary (vendor listings), thomann.co.uk/thomann_12h_ocarina_c3_violet_rivo.htm |
| Learning | A 12-hole is called beginner-friendly, about one hole per note. "It works much like a horizontal recorder", but the fingering is different and has to be memorised | secondary, instruments-du-monde.com/en/blogs/ocarina/how-many-holes-ocarina; data (a forum quote), forums.giantitp.com/archive/index.php/t-122611.html |
| Learning | Buyers' own words: "Is a 12 hole ocarina too complicated", "How hard is it to play the ocarina", "Is this an okay ocarina to learn" | data (thread titles), the giantitp forum and r/Ocarina mirrors |
| Learning | Sub-hole notes can sound low and weak (a buyer's review) | data (a review snippet), the Thomann listing |
| Printed vs ceramic | Prusa found the difference "really minor": PLA and ASA warmer, PETG brighter | secondary, vendor blog (Prusa, above) |

**What it means:** the scripts can show that it plays and what it is. "Afinada", "apta para la boca", "lavable",
"suena como cerámica" and any range stay off screen and out of the copy until the owner says how each piece is made and
tested.

## 2. Ocarina content that performs (`research-ocarina/02-formatos-reels.md`)

- **No viral printed-ocarina reel with measured views was found** (data gap). Instagram, TikTok and Shorts could not be
  searched from here.
- Instagram's top three Reels signals: watch time, likes per reach and **sends per reach**. Sends weigh a little more for
  non-followers (secondary, Mosseri via third parties: web.swipeinsight.app/posts/instagram-head-share-rates-crucial-for-broad-reach).
  This holds from our November research.
- What viewers say about printed instruments: tuning and a "grainy" finish, more often than amazement (opinion, forums:
  chiffandfipple.com/t/3d-printed-whistle/100647). Short "suena bien, fácil de tocar" praise appears on Printables model
  pages (snippet, printables.com/model/249011-single-print-ocarina/comments).
- **Music on business accounts:** the licensed music library is for personal use. Business accounts get Meta's Sound
  Collection (about 14,000 royalty-free tracks) (secondary, foximusic.com/blog/instagram-reels-music-copyright-legal-guide/;
  a Meta help snippet, help.instagram.com/ipad-app/402084904469945). Our own ambient bed and our own recording avoid the
  question.
- **What carries over to our toon reels** (opinion):
  - a real-sound proof insert with no music on top;
  - a tuner on screen, only if the tuning is tested;
  - a print that ends in its first note.
- **What needs a real person** (opinion): "day 1 vs day 100" and a close-up of someone playing. Real footage of the
  owner's hands is the closest we can get. It belongs in the proof, not the comedy.

## 4. Comedy formats (`research-ocarina/04-humor.md`)

- **Instrument comedy that travels** (secondary and opinion): a terrible first attempt, then a real tune; an animal
  answering; confident bad playing (TV Tropes, "Impractical Musical Instrument Skills"). A dog or a bird would be a third
  character, so the agent kept them off screen.
- **The ocarina's own story** (for a line, not a lecture):
  - Giuseppe Donati, Budrio, Italy. The date is tradition, not documented: 1853 or 1854 in a Lombardy heritage record,
    about 1860 at the Horniman Museum (snippets: lombardiabeniculturali.it/opere-arte/schede-complete/6c040-03451,
    horniman.ac.uk/object/M14-1990);
  - "ocarina" is Bolognese dialect for **"little goose"** (secondary, flutetunes.com/articles/ocarina/);
  - clay ocarinas and whistles in the Americas are thousands of years old (museum snippets: the Harvard Peabody,
    metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/502524).
- **Features a joke can prove** (true for 12-hole ocarinas in general; confirm for ours): it plays real tunes; each hole
  you uncover gives another note; the two small sub-holes add the lowest notes (secondary, vendor:
  pureocarinas.com/differance-10-hole-12-hole-ocarina, snippet); it fits in a hand (the owner's photos).
- **Not established:** "it doesn't break if you drop it". Ceramic breaks; PLA is brittle; no drop test of a printed
  ocarina was found. Only a filmed test by the owner could support it.
- **Franchise tropes to avoid** (the associations viewers make with the 12-hole shape, described generically):
  - a tune that changes the weather, the time of day or the light;
  - a tune that teleports, summons or opens something;
  - a hooded hero;
  - lifting the item to the camera in a "reveal" pose;
  - a chime that marks a solved puzzle.
- The agent's top concepts, which the plan builds on: "Primer intento" (a squeak, then a real tune), "Batata con
  agujeros" (counting the holes), "Regalo impaciente" (the 23 December date), "Ganso chiquito" (the name's story).

## 5. Sound (`research-ocarina/05-sonido.md`)

- **Honesty** (opinion, from law and policy): Ley 17.250 bans misleading advertising, omissions included (secondary,
  snippet: abogados.com.ar/inteligencia-artificial-y-publicidad/33536; the law's text at impo.com.uy did not open).
  Meta bans deceptive ads (secondary). **If a reel says or implies "así suena", the sound must be the shop's own ocarina.**
  The characters' synthetic voices are fine; a synthetic, stock or AI ocarina is not.
- **In tune:** about ±5 cents is the usual threshold, and about 10 cents is often audible (secondary, snippets:
  tunable.pages.dev/tuner/). Free tuner apps show the cents. Claim tuning only with the owner's real check.
- **Public-domain melodies** (the composition only; our own plain playing is safe, while arrangements and modern lyrics
  may not be):
  - **"Noche de paz"** (Gruber, 1818): public domain (data: hymnary.org/song/42/silent-night-holy-night). Its range is a
    tenth, so it fits an Alto C in C major (C5 to E6);
  - "Jingle Bells" (1857) and Beethoven's "Oda a la alegría": public domain (secondary);
  - unverified: "Campana sobre campana", "Los peces en el río", "Arroz con leche", "Adeste fideles";
  - **never:** "El tamborilero" (1941), "Feliz Navidad" (1970), "Blanca Navidad", any video-game theme.
- **Mixing:**
  - the ocarina's notes sit in the speech band, so it **never plays under a line**;
  - the ambient bed drops out while the ocarina plays;
  - about -14 LUFS for the whole reel, -1 dBTP (secondary; Meta publishes no figure);
  - little or no reverb on the ocarina (opinion).
- **Recording with a phone** (secondary, snippets: pureocarinas.com/how-to-record-an-ocarina; elgato.com): the phone
  about 30 cm away, above the ocarina and to one side of the window, never in front of the breath; a soft room; a
  10-second test for clipping on the high notes. Send it as a file, not a voice note (or by Drive if WhatsApp
  compresses it).
- "Original audio" might get reused by others, but no data says it helps reach (opinion).

## Rules that change (for the ocarina reels)

1. **Real sound only.** Every note the ocarina makes on screen comes from the owner's recording: the tunes, the scale,
   and the bad-note gag too. No synthetic, stock or AI ocarina. While it plays, a small badge says "Sonido real".
   **No recording, no reel.**
2. **The ocarina is the music when it plays.** The ambient bed fades out over about 0.5 s before the first note and
   returns after the last one. Never a spoken line, a subtitle-only line or an effect under a note. Little or no
   reverb. The whole reel at about -14 LUFS, -1 dBTP.
3. **The mouthpiece never reaches a face.** The ocarina plays by El Mago's magic: his gesture, and sparkles on the holes
   as they open. Papá Noel's own attempts are POV inserts: his mittens hold it at chest height and the mouthpiece leaves
   the bottom of the frame, with the character hidden while posed in place.
4. **Holes follow the real fingering.** The open and closed holes match the notes of the recording, from the owner's
   fingering chart (players will check). Without the chart, the sparkles circle the body and no single hole is shown
   lifting.
5. **Nothing in the world reacts to the music** except the characters' faces: no weather, light, time of day, doors,
   objects or animals. No hooded figure, no lifting the ocarina to the camera, no puzzle chime. Only public-domain or
   original melodies, played plainly by the owner. No franchise words or hashtags in captions.
6. **Proof has a sound.** After the joke and before the CTA: the owner's photo ("Así es de verdad"), then a real clip of
   the owner's hands playing, with its own sound ("Así suena de verdad"). If there is no video, the real audio plays over the
   photo.
7. **Claims wait for the owner:**
   - the key, the range, the size in cm and the colours;
   - "afinada": only if each piece is checked, and then exactly as the owner checks it (for example "la pruebo con afinador
     antes de entregarla");
   - never "apta para la boca", "irrompible", "suena como cerámica", "dos octavas" or "hecho a mano".
8. **The black piece needs light.** A matte black teardrop disappears in a dark set. Shoot it against a lit wall or the
   sky with a rim light, and keep it out of the night preset.

## Questions for the owner

1. **Photos:** the three photos of the ocarina, to attach in the session. A photo of it **in a hand** would prove the
   size without a number.
2. **Recording:** the checklist in Spanish is in `research-ocarina/05-sonido.md` and repeated in `PLAN_OCARINA.md`.
3. **Key and range:** Alto C? From which note to which?
4. **Material:** PLA or PETG? Does the mouthpiece get any treatment?
5. **Size** in cm, and the **colours** you can print it in.
6. **Tuning:** do you check each one with a tuner before delivery? How (which notes, how many cents)?
7. Does it come with a **fingering chart** or a **cord**?
8. **Lead time** for one ocarina, and a **Reyes** order date if you want a push for 6 January.
9. **The design:** is it your own model, or someone else's? If it is someone else's, what licence does it have?
   (MakerWorld's default licence is personal use only; see the skill's lessons.)
10. **Care:** what do you tell buyers about cleaning and heat (a car in the sun)?

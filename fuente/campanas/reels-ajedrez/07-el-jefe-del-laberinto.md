# Reel 7 · «El jefe del laberinto» (pelea contra el jefe final)

**Resumen:** un juego de acción de costado entre columnas griegas: un héroe dorado con piel de león avanza y aparece
el jefe final, un minotauro rojo enorme. Cuando chocan, el piso se vuelve un tablero de ajedrez y la escena termina
en el ajedrez de la antigua Grecia armado sobre su propio tablero, con muros de roca y un templo. Para fans de la
mitología y de los dioramas. 9:16, 10 s.

Sale del coleccionista de miniaturas (el ajedrez como pieza de exhibición y el tablero como diorama) y del creador de
TikTok (el «duelo» como gancho). Es el único set del catálogo que trae tablero: 52 × 50 cm, el dato del reel.

## PASO 2 · Generar el video completo con las imágenes

En Gemini, video **9:16 de 10 segundos**. Adjuntá las cuatro imágenes **en orden** (imagen 1, 2, 3 y 4) y pegá este
prompt:

```
Create one 10-second vertical 9:16 video using the four attached images as keyframes, in this order: Image 1 is the first frame (0 s), Image 2 appears at about 3 s, Image 3 at about 6 s, and Image 4 is the final frame (10 s). Keep exactly the look, characters, colors and pixel art style of the images.

Static camera in both scenes, no zoom, no pan, no depth of field, no motion blur. The change from Image 3 to Image 4 is one quick white flash with a pixel mosaic dissolve, like a 1990s game after a boss fight. Authentic 16-bit pixel art: hard square pixels, limited palette, low frame-rate sprite animation, flat 2D for the whole clip.

Action:
0 to 3 seconds (Image 1 to Image 2): the golden hero walks right with a two-frame walk cycle; the red eyes in the doorway blink; the ground shakes and the huge red minotaur stomps out, snorting steam.
3 to 5 seconds (Image 2): the screen shakes lightly with each of the minotaur's steps; the hero raises his club.
5 to 7.5 seconds (Image 2 to Image 3): both leap and clash in the middle with a burst of gold and red sparks; the marble floor flips tile by tile into a chessboard and small soldiers pop up in rows.
7.5 to 10 seconds (Image 4): white flash and mosaic dissolve to the printed Greek chess set on the table; a sparkle between the two kings. The last second holds still.

Audio: tense 16-bit boss music with a pounding drum loop, heavy chiptune footsteps, a bull snort, a whoosh for the leap, a big crunchy impact, quick flipping clicks as the tiles turn, a bright victory chord on the white flash, then a soft wooden clack. No dialogue, no voice, no music with lyrics.

No text, no letters, no numbers, no Greek inscriptions, no health bars, no subtitles, no captions, no logos anywhere in the video.
```

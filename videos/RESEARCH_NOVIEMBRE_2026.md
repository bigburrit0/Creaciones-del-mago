# November 2026: reels that sell (research and analysis)

Research team, 2026-11-07: five Haiku researchers, one angle each.
1. Reel structure and retention.
2. Sales funnel from a reel to a WhatsApp order.
3. November selling moments in Uruguay.
4. What 3D-printing and maker shops post that sells.
5. Persuasive copy and trend formats.

All five warned the same thing: most 2025-2026 sources are vendor and SEO blogs. The rules below say how solid each finding is, and that decides how much weight it carries in the scripts (`PLAN_NOVIEMBRE.md`).

## What the five reports agree on, ranked by how solid it is

| # | Finding | Evidence | What we do with it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Instagram's top three Reels signals are **watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach**. Sends weigh slightly more for people who do not follow you | Mosseri, January 2025, reported by four of the five researchers (buffer.com, dataslayer.ai, socialmediatoday.com). The "sends are 3-5× likes" multipliers have no primary source | Build for completion (short, a cut or a new thing every ~2 s, a payoff at the end), and give people a reason to **send it to a friend** |
| 2 | **Speech and on-screen text in the first 1-1.5 s**, with the promise or the result visible in frame 1 | Emplifi, second-hand: speech in the first 3 s gave about +25% 10-s retention compared with music only. Plus consensus across guides | Every reel opens with El Mago or Papá Noel talking from frame 1, a hook of six words or fewer on screen, and the products in shot |
| 3 | **CTAs usually cut reach. Comment prompts are the exception** | The only measured source: Fanpage Karma, 540 reels. Comment prompts gave about +43% reach per follower; "watch/participate" CTAs gave about -25% | **One** CTA per reel, as a comment: "Etiquetá a ese amigo", "Comentá FOTO". The WhatsApp number stays in the caption |
| 4 | **A mascot alone does not sell. The real product has to appear** | A 2025 experiment (n=203) and a CEO quote: a mascot drives recall, "not a direct sales driver" | Each reel ends on **real photos of the pieces** (`assets/p/*.webp`, the site's own product photos) before the CTA |
| 5 | **Process content and customisation** are the formats makers cite most for sales (layer by layer, "from your photo") | Maker blogs and one viral case (self-reported) | The layer-by-layer reveal is already our signature. Customisation gets its own reel |
| 6 | **Captions**: about 125 characters show before "más…"; keywords in the caption and on screen help Instagram search; professional accounts' reels can show up in Google (since July 2025) | Several guides. "Mosseri called keywords a primary signal" is unverified | The first 125 characters hold the product, "Montevideo" and the hook. Add alt text. Use 5-7 hashtags at most |
| 7 | **Length is unsettled**: one study (~6M reels) found 45-60 s best; other guides say 15-30 s | One secondary report against consensus | Keep 20-25 s, built for completion. Test longer cuts with **Trial Reels**, which show a reel to non-followers first |
| 8 | **Don't discount. Add value and real urgency**: bundles, free delivery, order cut-off dates, limited production slots. Never a fake countdown | Maker BFCM guides (opinion-level) | Use only true facts on screen: price and date before starting, no commitment; 48 h minimum; free delivery zones. Any offer (a pack, a cut-off date) is **your decision**: see the plan |

## Funnel (what goes where)

The researchers suggested a monthly mix of roughly **50% reach, 30% consideration and 20% conversion**. This is opinion, not data. The path is: reel, then a story the same day, then WhatsApp.

| Stage | The reel's job | CTA | Same-day story |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reach | Get sent to friends; get watched to the end | "Etiquetá a ese amigo" | Poll: "¿Cuál le regalás?" |
| Consideration | Show how a custom order works; answer "how much?" with "price before starting" | "Comentá FOTO" (reply by DM, then WhatsApp) | Question sticker "¿Qué foto me mandarías?" plus a WhatsApp link sticker |
| Conversion | Remove the last doubts (price, time, delivery, what to order), with honest urgency | "Escribime" | Link sticker "Pedí por WhatsApp"; a countdown sticker **to your own cut-off date** |

Comment keywords mean answering by hand, or with a tool such as ManyChat if you set one up. Meta only allows automated DMs that the user triggers.

Context: since May 2026, duty-free online imports are capped at USD 800 a year and three shipments (Cronista). The researcher's inference is that this may push gift buyers toward local makers. I would not claim it in a reel.

## Formats to use and avoid in November

- **Use:** identity lines that make people send the reel ("para el amigo que…"); objection busting (the questions customers really ask); transformation (photo to piece); layer-by-layer process; the click sound as ASMR; expectation vs. reality.
- **Careful:** "tell me without telling me" and other trends that are 3-4 weeks old are probably tired by now. "POV" still works.
- **Avoid:** fake discounts, fake countdowns, prices on screen (the price is sent on WhatsApp), and trademarked characters on screen. Three of the four past-order photos on the site show trademarks (One Piece twice, Peñarol), so the "pedidos reales" proof needs a photo of an original piece.

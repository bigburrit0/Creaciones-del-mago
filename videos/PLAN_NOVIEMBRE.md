proof (the site's photos of the real pieces) → one comment-type CTA.** The WhatsApp number goes in the caption, never in

## N1 "Un regalo para cada amigo" (reach, about 24 s, publish Fri 13 Nov)

**Idea.** A list of friend types, each matched to a real clicker or keychain from the catalogue. It is made to be sent: everyone has "el que no arranca sin café". Papá Noel plays two of the types, and the punchline is his.

**Why it sells.**
- It gives people a reason to send it, the strongest reach signal for non-followers.
- Each product appears with a reason to buy it for someone specific.
- It ends on the real photos.
- The CTA is a comment that tags a friend: a comment and a share at once.

# "Una más": script audit (2026-11-08)

The first draft is reel 2 in `../PLAN_AJEDREZ.md` (2026-11-07). I audited it against:
- `../RESEARCH_AJEDREZ_2_2026.md`: the customer, the competition, gift psychology and the critic;
- the synthesis rules in `../RESEARCH_AJEDREZ_2026.md`;
- the skill's lessons.

## Checklist

| Check | First draft | Verdict | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook in 1.5 s, product in frame 1 | A dragon head growing on a printer; text "32 piezas"; line "Una pieza más y paramos" | **Fails for non-players** (critic 1): nothing says what it is | Frame 1 names it in words: "Ajedrez vikingo impreso en 3D", plus a counter "1/32". Line: "Treinta y dos piezas. ¡A imprimir!" (the brand's catchphrase) |
| One joke | "Una más" plus the count plus an unplugged printer that still prints | **Two jokes, one broken** (critic 5): unplugged printing reads as a glitch, and the puff as smoke | One joke: Papá Noel keeps asking "¿Ya está?", El Mago keeps answering "Una más", while the hours pass. The unplug and the puff are removed |
| The work shown honestly (labour illusion; new) | A fast montage | **Partly**: work shown, time not | The time passes visibly: day → evening → night → dawn while the board fills piece by piece. Papá Noel falls asleep standing up. No failed prints, no stalling (Morales 2005: padding kills the effect) |
| The product after the joke | The photos after the unplug | Keep, with the button reworked | After 32/32, a 33rd piece prints. El Mago: "Y si se te pierde una… ¡una más!", a real service (a lost piece is reprinted from photos and measurements). Then the real photos |
| Real proof | Real photos | **Keep** | Viking and medieval photos, **shown before the CTA** asks "¿vikingo o medieval?" (critic 3) |
| ONE comment CTA | "¿Medieval o vikingo? Comentá cuál y te mando las fotos" | **Weak reward** (critic 4) | "¿Vikingo o medieval? Comentá cuál y te paso precio y fecha." A real reward; price stays off screen |
| Honest time claims | On screen: "Un juego completo lleva muchas horas de impresora" | Keep the fact, change the frame (critic 6) | Told by the light and the sleeping Papá Noel; the caption says it as care ("pieza por pieza, a pedido"). Never "48 h" |
| Per-side colours (competition gap; new) | — | Missing | The set prints in **two colours, one per side** (bronze and silver); the caption names the choice |
| The loop | Back to the printer | **Dead air** (critic 8) | The last frame is the full board with the printer behind it. The first frame is the empty board and the first piece printing |
| Length | 22 s | OK | Target 22-25 s after measuring the voices |
| Cast and repetition with reel 1 | Same table, same pair | Accepted for continuity (a series); new look | Night lighting, Papá Noel asleep, the board filling from empty |

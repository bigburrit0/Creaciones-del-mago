# 03 - Gift occasions and buyer personas: 12-hole ocarina, Uruguay, Nov 2026 to Reyes 2027

Scope: the window from late November 2026 to 6 January 2027. Dates from the earlier shop research are used as given. Evidence labels follow CONTEXT.md. Almost every Uruguayan press source was reachable only as a search snippet, because news domains did not resolve from this session. Those items are marked "(snippet)".

## 1. Reyes / 6 January in Uruguay

- **6 January is a working holiday that is not moved.** Calendar pages list 6 January 2026 as a "feriado laborable" that is not shifted to another day. [secondary] https://datosuruguay.com/feriados ; https://www.jibble.io/es/recursos/dias-feriados-uruguay-2026 ; https://www.elobservador.com.uy/nacional/969 (snippet)
- **Naming is unclear.** One source says the date was renamed "Día de los Niños" and that calendars show it as "Día de Reyes o Día de los Niños". [secondary, unverified] Not confirmed by any official Uruguayan source. Do not use the name in reels until it is checked.
- **The official children's day is in August.** Uruguay's Día de la Niñez falls on a Sunday in August, normally the second or third one. Telenoche and Ámbito give 2024 and 2025 as the third Sunday and 2026 as 16 August. Sources disagree on 2026, so check it. [secondary] https://www.telenoche.com.uy/domingo-a1067 ; https://www.lacapital.com.ar/informacion-general/cuando-es-el-dia-del-nino-y-que-este-ano-habra-fin-semana-largo-n10273018/amp (Argentine, mentions Uruguay's date) (snippet)
- **Size of the kids' gift moment.** The clearest Uruguayan figure is for August, not Reyes: Ámbito reports toy demand up about 20% for Día del Niño and an average gift spend of USD 74 [secondary, snippet] https://www.ambito.com/uruguay/dia-del-nino-las-compras-impulsan-el-comercio-internacional-y-crece-20-las-ventas-juguetes-n6307766/amp . El Observador (2022) calls Día del Niño one of the most lucrative sales periods, behind only Christmas and Reyes [secondary, snippet] https://www.elobservador.com.uy/nota/semana-de-oro-en-las-jugueterias-por-el-dia-del-nino-cuanto-gastan-y-que-compran-los-uruguayos--202289154431/amp . Reyes is therefore a real but secondary kids' date in Uruguay. Christmas outranks it.
- **Christmas framing.** Bloomberg Línea says Uruguay is the only Latin American country that does not officially celebrate Christmas, and that 24 December is "Día de la Familia" under a 1919 law. It also reports that 70% of Uruguayans buy gifts for traditional festivities, and 87% actually bought one in 2023 [secondary, snippet] https://www.bloomberglinea.com/latinoamerica/los-regalos-que-mas-compran-en-navidad-los-paises-de-latam-y-cuanto-dinero-destinan/ ; see also https://www.minutouno.com/mundo/por-que-uruguay-no-celebra-oficialmente-la-navidad-y-que-se-conmemora-el-25-diciembre-n6227787 (snippet).
- **Argentina, for context only.** Reyes sales there rose by under 1% in units or value. Nothing is known about Uruguay's Reyes sales for 2025-26. [secondary, not Uruguay] https://www.diariojornada.com.ar/408981/economia/reyes_magos_las_ventas_aumentaron_por_debajo_del_1 (snippet)
- **Shopping timing.** No Uruguayan source found. [opinion] Families likely buy for Reyes in the first days of January, after aguinaldo (paid around 18 December).

## 2. Music in Uruguayan schools

- **No source confirms flauta dulce in Uruguayan public primary curricula.** Every flauta dulce result is from Spain or elsewhere. [data gap] https://digitum.um.es/digitum/bitstream/10201/114107/1/LA-FLAUTA-DULCE-EN-EDUCACIO%cc%81N-PRIMARIA.-ESTUDIO-DE-CASO-SOBRE-LA-EFICACIA-DE-DIFERENTES-METODOLOGI%cc%81AS-PARA-EL-AULA.pdf (Spanish study, not Uruguayan)
- **Public art and music programmes exist.** The Escuelas de Educación Artística of the CEIP date from 1942 and aim to teach artistic knowledge in public primary schools [secondary] https://www.gub.uy/ministerio-desarrollo-social/node/8864 . A Presidencia notice describes ANEP and SODRE music initiation, orchestra and choir classes for children in Casavalle [secondary] https://www.gub.uy/presidencia/comunicacion/noticias/2000-estudiantes-casavalle-accederan-clases-iniciacion-musical-sinfonica-coro (snippet).
- **A thesis on music teaching in DGEIP primary schools.** It looks at who teaches music in public primary schools and how they are trained. The full text was not read. [secondary] https://rad.ort.edu.uy/bitstreams/95cc7cbe-b902-4901-9c9a-1dfb3f16550a/download (snippet)
- **No ocarina presence in schools, Plan Ceibal or conservatoires was found.** [data gap]
- **Why it matters.** [opinion] A child who knows flauta dulce already understands finger covering and breath control, so the 12-hole ocarina can be pitched as "the one you already know, with a different sound". Confirm with a teacher or the CEIP music programme before using that line in a reel.

## 3. Personas

The strongest evidence is for gamers and gifts. Adult learners and camping come from sellers and blogs. The personas below are [opinion] unless noted.

1. **Grandparent or parent buying for a child who plays flauta dulce.** Trigger: the child is already in music class; the teacher mentions the recorder. Worry: "Will it be too hard, and will it break?" Date: early December (amigo invisible in families) or 6 January. Support: a trade report says parents look for an inexpensive, easy instrument for children [secondary] (gamingnexus.com gift guide; URL withheld because it names the franchise)
2. **Parent of a child who has not started music.** Trigger: the school year ends (18 December). Worry: "Will the child stop after a week?" Date: 16 December. [opinion]
3. **Teen or adult gamer, buying for self or a sibling.** Demand comes from [the franchise] association. Worry: is it a real instrument, plastic or ceramic, and will it play in tune? Date: Black Friday (27 Nov) or a pre-announcement. Support: a 2012 gamer-gift listing (snippet) https://www.trendhunter.com/trends/louis-ocarina ; a blog says many players come to the hobby after playing that game [secondary] https://pureocarinas.com/challenges-marketing-the-ocarina
4. **Adult who always wanted to learn an instrument.** Trigger: a New Year resolution. Worry: "Am I too old?" A beginner FAQ says you can start at any age [secondary] https://geekchamp.com/feature-30-years-later-im-finally-learning-to-play-the-ocarina-and-you-should-too/ . Date: 3 January 2027, after Reyes.
5. **Traveller, camper or beach-goer.** Trigger: summer holidays (20 Dec to 15 Jan). Worry: fragility and size. A buyer said they bought one to take outdoors [secondary, anecdotal] https://www.shopsavvy.com/best-picks/best-ocarina-flutes (reviews). Date: 21 December.
6. **Amigo invisible at work or with friends.** Trigger: the first week of December. Worry: "What if they do not like it?" Budget: corporate gifts in Uruguay cluster around UYU 2,000-3,000, with 45% in that range [secondary, snippet] https://www.elobservador.com.uy/fiestas-la-oficina-2026-crece-la-tendencia-del-regalo-corporativo-digital-uruguay-n6062456 . The amigo invisible is a popular custom [secondary, snippet] https://www.montevideo.com.uy/Tiempo-libre/Cinco-razones-para-organizar-un-Amigo-Invisible-este-fin-de-ano-uc944286 . Date: 1 December.
7. **Teacher (end-of-year gift).** Trigger: the last day of classes for Inicial and Primaria (18 December). Worry: "Will it be useful in the classroom?" Date: 16 December. [opinion]
8. **Musician or collector.** Worry: tuning and tone. Reviews recommend 12-hole C models for beginners and say the highest notes can sound breathy [secondary] https://www.shopsavvy.com/best-picks/best-ocarina-flutes . Not a reel persona: use for detailed posts.

## 4. The market in Uruguay

- **Mercado Libre Uruguay was not reachable.** [limit] Search surfaced one 12-hole, PLA, [the franchise]-style listing sold through a reseller with a US$95.31 price. It is an import, not a local maker [secondary] (latinafy.com product page; URL withheld because it names the franchise) . Use the price for internal context only, never in reels.
- **Imports on local shops.** Desertcart Uruguay lists imported ceramic 12-hole models, sold with shipping and customs handled [secondary, snippet] https://www.desertcart.uy/products/32646756 (listing ID not confirmed).
- **Listings emphasise** gamers, gifts and ease of learning. One seller says the fingering is close to a keyboard [secondary, snippet] (gamingnexus.com gift guide; URL withheld because it names the franchise) . Sellers also stress an easy first song.
- **Artisans.** Punta del Diablo's artisans sell on the Avenida de los Pescadores. A 2021 El Observador article covers the dispute over a craft fair [secondary, old] https://www.elobservador.com.uy/nota/-los-hippies-murieron-en-los-60-la-lucha-de-los-artesanos-de-punta-del-diablo-202111516190/amp . No ocarina seller was found there, in the Rambla or at Tristán Narvaja.
- **Gap.** [data gap] No local ocarina maker, no Uruguayan sales figures and no evidence of Ferias de Navidad stalls.

## 5. Summer and outdoors

- No Uruguayan source ties ocarinas to the beach, the Rambla, Punta del Diablo or camping. [data gap]
- One buyer said they chose it for outdoor hobby use [secondary, anecdotal] (see persona 5).
- [opinion] The fit is plausible for fogón and beach evenings, where a small, quiet instrument suits a campfire. Test this with a single reel before building a campaign on it.

**Reyes push after 24 December.** [opinion] Possible, but thin. A single post on Sun 3 January 2027 aimed at persona 4 (New Year resolution) fits better than a Reyes-for-kids reel, because the Uruguayan kids' gift peak is in August. Do not promise delivery dates.

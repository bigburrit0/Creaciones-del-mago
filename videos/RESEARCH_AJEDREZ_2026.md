Three teams of three Haiku researchers worked on 2026-11-07:

| Team | Members | Covered |
|---|---|---|
| Marketing | A1, A2, A3 | Buyers, history as a hook, funnel and an audit of `RESEARCH_NOVIEMBRE_2026.md` |

Sources are mostly blogs and search snippets. Several primary hosts (Instagram's creator site, museums, chess.com) did not resolve from the session, so each finding is marked as **data**, **secondary** or **opinion**. The rules in "Synthesis" are what the scripts in `PLAN_AJEDREZ.md` follow.

## The product, as the site states it (`fuente/guias/06`, `07`, `09`)

- **Themes:** medieval (castle rooks; the detailed version has knights in armour with heraldic shields, mitred bishops with crosiers, barded horses) and viking (a king in furs with a sword, a shieldmaiden queen with a round shield, a bear-hooded seer as bishop, a dragon-prow knight, a stave-church rook). The photos are `assets/p/medieval.webp`, `medieval-p.webp` and `vikingos.webp`.
- **What the customer chooses:** the colours of each side (up to 4 colours per piece), the size (up to 25 cm per piece; tournament king 9.5 cm), PLA or PETG, and the theme.
- **The board is separate.** Almost every set is the pieces only.
- **Price:** it comes from weight and print time. A full set takes many printer hours (the medieval set, more than 10). Price and date go by WhatsApp before starting, no commitment.
- **Playable:** the medieval set (and the minimalist one), near tournament size, are for playing. Thin, very detailed designs are more for display.
- **Lost piece:** a replacement can be printed from photos and measurements of the others.

**Never on screen:** a price, "48 h" for a whole set, or a cut-off date the shop has not confirmed.

## Marketing team

**A3: audit of the November research.**
- **Keep:**
  - The ranking signals Mosseri named in January 2025: watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach (secondary sources).
  - Trial Reels go to non-followers first ([about.fb.com, Dec 2024](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers/)).
  - Public reels from professional accounts can appear in Google since July 2025 (secondary).
- **Correct:**
  - CTAs overall cost about 11% of reach (Fanpage Karma, 540 reels, correlational). **Comment prompts (+43%) and like prompts (about +86%) gain reach**; watch/join prompts lose about 25% ([blog.fanpagekarma.com/?p=22627](https://blog.fanpagekarma.com/?p=22627)).
  - "48 h" is the minimum lead time, not the time to print a set.
  - The cut-off dates (9 and 14 Dec) were placeholders.
  - Meta allows **one private reply per comment, within 7 days**; once the person replies, a 24 h window opens. No cold DMs.
  - Fake urgency can be illegal: Uruguay's consumer law, Ley 17.250, prohibits misleading advertising (general text, not checked on IMPO). The FTC treats baseless countdowns as deceptive.
- **Drop:**
  - "Keywords are a primary signal."
  - "45-60 s is best."
  - "Sends weigh 3-5× likes."
  - "The import cap pushes buyers to local makers."
  - Keep the 50/30/20 content mix, labelled as opinion.

**A1: buyers.**
- **Demand:**
  - Chess.com passed 250M members in Feb 2026 and reported 267M+ in Q2 2026, +27.5% year on year (chess.com news and board report, snippets).
  - Montevideo hosted the 2026 South American championship for the Uruguayan federation's 100th anniversary (fideamerica.net).
  - Uruguayan school-programme counts conflict (65-128 schools). **Do not quote a number.**
- **Personas:**

  | Persona | Trigger | Objection | Proof that removes it |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Gift giver | Christmas, Reyes, Día del Padre (2nd Sunday of July, so Sun 11 Jul 2027), "the one who has everything" | "¿Llega a tiempo? ¿Es como la foto?" | Real photos; the date before starting |
  | Parent or player | A kid is learning | "¿Para jugar o para mirar? ¿Aguanta?" | Tournament size; PETG; a real game |
  | Decor or collector | — | Price against a shop set | Colour choice; the detail; why it takes hours |

- **Prices seen** (Etsy snapshots, only a reference):
  - a 16-piece viking set without board, about CA$27;
  - a printed medieval decor set, about CA$60;
  - handmade gothic sets, CA$192-250.
- **Material** (vendor sources):
  - PLA softens at about 55-65 °C, PETG at about 75-85 °C.
  - A set that lives in sun or a car should be PETG.

**A2: history as a hook.**
- **The Lewis chessmen:**
  - found in 1831 on Lewis, Scotland;
  - more than 80 pieces, in the British Museum and National Museums Scotland;
  - 12th century;
  - some rooks are berserkers biting their shields.
- **How to say it:**
  - The queens only "parecen preocupadas": the museums do not say they are worried.
  - Origin is usually given as Norway, but debated (Iceland is also proposed).
- **Hnefatafl:** no viking rulebook survives.
- **Staunton:** registered on 1 March 1849, with the designer's name misspelled "Cook".

**Our sets are not Lewis replicas.** The viking rook is a stave church, so a history hook must say "inspired by", never show a piece we don't sell.

Content angles, ranked by sends and sales (opinion):
1. **Vikingos vs. caballeros**: an identity question that gets people tagging the friend on the other side.
2. **The history hook**: 1831.
3. **The worried queen**: meme-friendly.
4. **The same knight in four colourways**: sells to decor buyers.
5. **The heavy-piece ASMR**: watch time, weak sales on its own.
6. **Hnefatafl**: niche.

## Humor and script team

**B1: chess humor.**
- Jokes non-players also get:
  - the knight moving in an L (best as a physical hop);
  - "the queen does everything, the king hides";
  - "it's a rook, not a castle".
- Player-only jokes: "Google en passant", the bongcloud. Never name real players.
- Wordplay:
  - **"Jaque mate… ¿y un mate?"** (checkmate and the drink);
  - "El Mago no mueve ficha: la imprime";
  - "El caballo va en L, como vos cuando llegás tarde".
- "Te comí la reina" and "peón de la vida" were not found as established slang. Avoid them.

**B3: script craft.**
- **Hook:** 6-12 spoken words, 3-8 on screen, a specific promise, no warm-up.
- **Shape:**
  - One idea per reel.
  - Rule of three.
  - A 0.5-1 s reaction after the punchline.
  - The product comes after the joke, never before.
  - One CTA, a choice question ("¿vikingo o caballero?") rather than "¿qué opinan?".
- **Pace:** 2.5-3 words per second for TTS (Spanish at a fast read is about 3.6), and measure the take.
- **Loop:** the last frame echoes the first.

The 12 mistakes to avoid:
1. A warm-up hook.
2. A hook that promises more than the reel delivers.
3. More than 3 words per second.
4. Two jokes in one reel.
5. A punchline with no reaction.
6. The product shown before the payoff.
7. Stacked CTAs.
8. A generic CTA.
9. No loop.
10. A character out of voice.
11. Subtitles timed by eye.
12. Text too small or too long.

## Editing team

**C1: pacing.**
- Average cut every 1.2-2.5 s, alternating 1 s bursts with 2.5-3 s holds.
- Pause 0.5-0.8 s before a punchline. The punchline gets the longest shot (2 s or more), then cut straight to the reaction.
- Proof shots at 1-1.5 s each.
- Card 2.5-3 s.

Instagram's own pages name full watch-through and the 3-second skip rate as key (creators.instagram.com, secondary).

22 s template:

| Time (s) | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0-1.5 | Hook |
| 1.5-5 | Setup, 3 shots |
| 5-9.5 | Escalation, 3 gags of about 1.5 s |
| 9.5-10 | A beat of silence |
| 10-13 | Payoff, then the reaction |
| 13-17.5 | Proof: photos, then the print |
| 17.5-19.5 | CTA |
| 19.5-22 | Card |

**C2: small objects made heroic.**
- **Shooting:**
  - Low angles (a worm's-eye view of the dragon prow), slow push-ins, level orbits (the pieces are symmetrical).
  - Key light at 45°, rim light behind and to the side. A flat front light kills the detail.
  - Shallow focus reads as "miniature", but blur alone looks like a camera fault.
  - Two colours lit warm against cool for a duel.
- **Reveals:** a battle formation (pieces sliding into their squares, rank by rank), the "forge" print (layer by layer with a warm glow), a cloth pull.

**C3: sound and text.**
- **Loudness:** -14 LUFS with a -1 dBTP ceiling is the community target. There is no Meta spec.
- **Music:** our own synthesised music avoids licence risk, and business accounts get a smaller audio library anyway.
- **The clack of a piece:** a 5 ms noise burst, a 1.8 kHz ping decaying in 30 ms, and a wooden body at about 400 Hz.
- **Medieval palette:** D Dorian, 96-104 bpm, drone, lute arpeggio, a recorder-like lead, a frame drum, a bell for check.
- **Viking palette:** E Aeolian, 116-124 bpm, a lur call, a drum and a string ostinato.
- **SFX:** at most one per 1.5 s, never on a voice onset. No ambience loop (the buzz lesson).
- **Text:**
  - 2-2.5 words per second, at most 2 lines of about 30 characters.
  - Each word on screen for at least 0.3-0.4 s; each caption block at least 1 s.
  - Hook title of 6 words or fewer, on screen 2.5-3.5 s; card at least 3 s.
  - Cover from frame 1-2 s, with the title and no subtitles.

## Synthesis: the rules for the chess series

1. **One identity question carries the series:** "¿Vikingo o caballero?". It is a choice CTA (comments), it is shareable (people tag the other side), and it sells both best sellers. The rest of the series uses it as a callback.
2. **Show only real pieces from our sets**, modelled from the photos (`biblioteca/3d/web/ajedrez3d.js`). Lewis facts appear only as "inspired by" hooks, and the board is never shown as included.
3. **Comedy is physical and visual:** pieces hop, slide, tip over and turn, with no hands touching pieces. The knight's L, the king tipping over for checkmate, the printer's "una más", Papá Noel misunderstanding "mate".
4. **Each reel has one joke, then the product** (real photos), then ONE comment CTA with its keyword, and a DM flow by private reply.
5. **Honest urgency only:**
   - "Te paso precio y fecha antes de empezar."
   - "Un juego completo lleva muchas horas de impresora: pedilo con tiempo."
   - A dated cut-off only after the shop confirms it.
6. **Timing:**
   - hook 1.5 s;
   - cuts every 1.2-2.5 s;
   - a 0.5 s beat before the punchline, a hold of at least 2 s on it;
   - proof 1-1.5 s per photo;
   - card about 3 s;
   - the last frame echoes the first.
7. **Sound:**
   - a different musical palette per side, medieval (Dorian, lute and bell) and viking (Aeolian, lur and drum);
   - a wooden clack for every move;
   - no ambience;
   - final mix at -14 LUFS.

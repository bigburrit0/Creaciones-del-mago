## 2. Local references (Uruguay, Río de la Plata)

- **Summer heat**: a forecast of a hotter-than-normal summer with several heat waves (montevideo.com.uy, date to
  check against Inumet). Pocitos and the rambla packed during heat waves. Evergreen and low risk: **the best local hook**.
- **Carnaval 2027**: Prueba de Admisión 10–16 Nov 2026 (34 groups, montevideo.com.uy). Carnival Monday/Tuesday
  8–9 Feb 2027 (computed from Easter; decree not out). Llamadas usually the first Thursday/Friday of February (2027
  dates not confirmed). Strong identity, but the product hook is weak, and candombe and Llamadas are Afro-Uruguayan
  heritage, so any use must be respectful and not a costume gag.
- **Football**: Uruguay went out in the group stage of the 2026 World Cup; Argentina lost the final to Spain
  (19 Jul 2026, sources disagree on the score). Fan wounds: **avoid**, or self-deprecating only.
- **Evergreen Uruguayan humour**: waiting for the ómnibus, the wind, the feria, fainá and chivito, UTE/OSE bills,
  "frío en pleno verano". No 2026 source needed.

## 3. Products and safe designs

- Trending per sources (no sales data): **articulated/flexi animals**, fidget and kinetic print-in-place toys,
  modular desk storage, **personalised name items** (keychains, tags), blind-box mini packs, phone stands.
- Uruguay summer to March: mate accessories (personalised bombilla holders, posamates), beach-bag keychains, **name
  tags for school backpacks**, pencil holders.
- **Licences**: MakerWorld's default licence is personal use only; CC-BY allows selling with credit; NC licences don't.
  Minecraft, Pokémon, One Piece and Star Wars are trademarks: in NEW reels show original or commercially licensed
  designs. Sources: blog.bambulab.com (commercial licence membership), makers101.com.
